Obsidian Sticky installer

Flash the voice-notes firmware onto a Seeed reTerminal Sticky straight from this page. Version ….

Your browser cannot talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer, with the Sticky plugged in over USB-C.

Web Serial needs a secure connection. Open this page over https:// in Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer.

Four steps

  1. Plug the Sticky into your computer over USB-C. Pick the port your system lists as CH343 when the browser asks.
  2. Click Install above and let it write. No button combination is needed, the bridge resets the chip by itself.
  3. On your phone or laptop, join the open Wi-Fi network named Sticky-Setup.
  4. Open http://192.168.4.1 and fill in your Wi-Fi, speech-to-text and Obsidian details, then press Save and restart.
